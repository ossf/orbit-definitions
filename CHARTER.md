# Charter

The ORBIT Definitions SIG is governed by the [Definitions SIG Parent Charter](https://github.com/ossf/wg-orbit/blob/main/technical-initiatives/sigs/definitions-sig-charter.md), maintained in the [ORBIT Working Group repository](https://github.com/ossf/wg-orbit) alongside the [ORBIT WG Charter](https://github.com/ossf/wg-orbit/blob/main/CHARTER.md).

Those documents take precedence over anything in this repository. Where a policy here conflicts with them, the parent charter governs and the local policy is void to the extent of the conflict.
